### Verified

- Five failed sends at base 30 s: claims at about 0, 30, 90, 210 and 450 s, attempts 1 to 5, waits 30/60/120/240 s, then the fifth failure sets `dead`. This matches R10 and the formula.
- Admin retry resets `attempts` and `next_attempt_at`, and only from `failed` or `dead`, which matches R14.
- R1–R9 and R12–R18 are covered, apart from F2/F10 for R10/R11 and F5 for N3. N2, N4 and N5 are covered.
- Packages, checked with `npm view` and the package sources:
  - `@fastify/jwt` 10.2.9, `@fastify/cookie` 11.1.5 and `@fastify/rate-limit` 11.3.0 each declare `fastify: '5.x'`.
  - `fastify-type-provider-zod` 7.0.0 needs fastify ^5.5.0 and zod >=4.1.5, which is zod 4. Latest zod is 4.6.7. It also has non-optional peers `@fastify/swagger` and `openapi-types`.
  - `drizzle-orm` 0.45.4 accepts `pg` >=8 and `postgres` >=3. Current versions are 8.23.1 and 4.1.1.
  - `nodemailer` 10.6.1 needs Node >=20.
  - Nx 24.0.7 and spartan-ng 1.10.1 accept Angular 22 (D-008, D-009).
- Node 26.13.1: `fetch` with `redirect:'error'` throws on a 302 with cause "unexpected redirect". Tested against a local server.
- `new URL()` rejects the hostile test URLs from the design and collapses `..` and `%2e%2e`.
- The Mailpit swagger lists `GET` and `DELETE /api/v1/messages`.
- AGENTS.md, N4 and D-019 agree on `SLACK_WEBHOOK_ALLOWED_PREFIXES`. The cut list is the same in D-014 and the plan.
